# Nebula: The Space Symphony

> A sci-fi rhythm game where the music you play shapes the universe around you.

**[Play on itch.io](https://galaxyco.itch.io/nebula-the-space-symphony)** · Built with Unity · College Project

---

## What is it?

Nebula is a 3D rhythm game with a twist — hitting notes doesn't just score points, it *builds a solar system*. Every note you match fills up the "Galaxy Energy" bar, which your character Nebula uses to create space objects in real time: planets, stars, entire celestial bodies. Miss notes and the energy drops, leaving your creations incomplete.

You play as Nebula, a Space Elf with a mysterious connection to the Galactic force. She carries a cosmic keyboard that floats around her, gathering and channelling energy as you play. The better you perform musically, the more detailed and alive the world around you becomes.

## My Role

I was the **sole programmer** on a team of 5. My teammates handled 3D assets, 2D art, sound design, and VFX. Everything in the codebase — all gameplay systems, mechanics, UI, shaders — was designed and built by me.

**Systems I built:**

- Rhythm engine — note detection, timing windows, and accuracy scoring
- Galaxy Energy system — ties musical performance to in-game creation mechanics
- Procedural space object generation triggered by player performance
- Physics-based collision and 3D environment interaction
- Multi-scene architecture across game levels
- Save/load system for persistent player progress
- Full UI/HUD — energy bars, menus, in-game overlays
- Custom shaders and visual effects in ShaderLab and HLSL
- Integration of team-provided VFX and 3D assets into gameplay systems
